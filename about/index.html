About Supplementopedia

Supplementopedia is a supplement ingredient encyclopedia and blog. It exists because most supplement websites are storefronts disguised as information.

What this site is

An ingredient-first reference. Each ingredient page covers what a supplement does, what the research actually shows, common dosages, and things to watch out for. Blog posts answer real questions sourced from supplement communities online.

What this site isn't

This is not a product review site. There are no "best magnesium supplement" roundups with affiliate links to Amazon. No sponsored content. No brand partnerships influencing what gets written.

How it works

Blog posts are the front door — they answer questions people are actually searching for. Each post links to relevant ingredient pages, which serve as the reference backbone. The ingredient catalog grows organically as new topics demand new pages.

Who's behind this

One person building a supplement reference that's actually useful. If you have questions or feedback, the best way to reach out is through the site's social channels.
