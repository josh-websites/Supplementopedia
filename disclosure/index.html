How This Site Is Supported

Supplementopedia is an independently run informational website. To help cover hosting and operating costs, this site participates in affiliate advertising programs. This means we may earn a commission when you click on certain banner links and make a purchase — at no additional cost to you.

Our Affiliate Partners

Supplementopedia currently works with two affiliate partners:

  • Herbs Pro — an online retailer of vitamins, herbs, and health products
  • HealthLabs.com — an online service for ordering lab tests without a doctor visit

These relationships are managed through the CJ Affiliate (Commission Junction) network.

How Affiliate Links Appear

Affiliate links on this site appear only as banner advertisements — clearly separated from editorial content. We do not use inline product affiliate links within articles or ingredient pages. You will never find a supplement recommendation on this site that links directly to a product purchase page.

Editorial Independence

Affiliate relationships do not influence our content. Supplementopedia does not:

  • Recommend specific supplement products or brands within articles
  • Write content to promote affiliate partner products
  • Alter research summaries or ingredient information to favor any commercial interest
  • Accept payment for editorial coverage

The information on this site is based on publicly available research and is written independently of any commercial consideration.

FTC Compliance

This disclosure is provided in accordance with the Federal Trade Commission's guidelines on endorsements and testimonials (16 CFR Part 255). We believe in full transparency about how this site generates revenue.

Questions
